### 📖 Bob Tavsifi
KUKA RSI real vaqtli interfeysi, ROS2 drayverlari, MoveIt2 to'siqlarni aylanib o'tish va Computer Vision (PnP, Hand-Eye AX=XB) integratsiyasi.

Bu repozitoriy **KUKA Industrial Robotics Course** o'quv dasturining bir qismi bo'lib, quyidagi darslarni o'z ichiga oladi:

### 📑 Darslar Mundarijasi
* [**20-kuka-rsi-interface**: KUKA RSI (Robot Sensor Interface) real-time interfeysi](./20-kuka-rsi-interface) — *KUKA RSI arxitekturasi: tashqi kompyuterdan real vaqtda (<12ms siklda) XML paketlar protokoli orqali to'g'ridan-to'g'ri boshqaruv.*
* [**21-ros2-driver-integration**: ROS 2 drayverlari integratsiyasi va RViz vizuallashtirish](./21-ros2-driver-integration) — *ROS 2 (Humble/Iron) arxitekturasi, rasmiy kuka_robot_drivers, robot holatini o'qish va RViz muhitida 3D URDF modelini harakatlantirish.*
* [**22-moveit2-trajectory-planning**: MoveIt 2 yordamida to'siqlarni aylanib o'tuvchi harakat rejalashtirish](./22-moveit2-trajectory-planning) — *MoveIt 2 kutubxonasi, OMPL rejalashtiruvchilari, dinamik paydo bo'lgan to'siqlarni chetlab o'tish (Collision Avoidance).*
* [**23-computer-vision-pick-and-place**: Computer Vision va Hand-Eye kalibratsiyasi (Pick-and-Place)](./23-computer-vision-pick-and-place) — *Pinhole kamera modeli, OpenCV cv2.solvePnP, cv2.Rodrigues, Hand-Eye AX=XB (Tsai-Lenz) kalibratsiyasi va robotlashtirilgan saralash.*
