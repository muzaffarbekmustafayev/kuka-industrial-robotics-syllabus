### 📖 Dars Haqida (Overview)
ROS 2 (Humble/Iron) arxitekturasi, rasmiy kuka_robot_drivers, robot holatini o'qish va RViz muhitida 3D URDF modelini harakatlantirish.

* **Texnologiyalar:** `ROS 2 Humble, kuka_robot_drivers, URDF, RViz2`
* **Asosiy Manipulyator:** KUKA KR 6 R900-2 (KR AGILUS-2)

### 🎯 Laboratoriya Topshirig'i (Lab Assignment)
