### 📖 Bob Tavsifi
Murakkab 3D choklar bo'ylab payvandlash/yelimlash traektoriyalari, konveyerga moslashuvchi (Conveyor Tracking) palletizatsiya va saralash.

Bu repozitoriy **KUKA Industrial Robotics Course** o'quv dasturining bir qismi bo'lib, quyidagi darslarni o'z ichiga oladi:

### 📑 Darslar Mundarijasi
* [**24-trajectory-welding-and-glueing**: Payvandlash va yelim surtish traektoriyalarini dasturlash](./24-trajectory-welding-and-glueing) — *Payvandlash (Welding) va yelimlash (Glueing) texnologik talablari. Doimiy chiziqli tezlikda murakkab fazoviy 3D choklar bo'ylab harakat.*
* [**25-palletizing-and-sorting-system**: Palletizatsiya va konveyerga dinamik moslashuvchi saralash tizimi](./25-palletizing-and-sorting-system) — *Palletizatsiya va konveyer harakat tezligiga moslashuvchi (Conveyor Tracking) aqlli saralash tizimi. KRL va Python aralash master-loyihasi.*
